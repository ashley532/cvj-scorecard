📖 What these mean

Opt-ins / week ★ (North-Star) — new gated manifesto opt-ins captured in the last 7 days (rolling). Leading indicator of awareness → captured lead. GHL.
Ad spend /30d — total paid ad spend across the Meta ad account, last 30 days.
Cold-manifesto CPL — Cost Per Lead on the cold manifesto campaign = spend ÷ new leads (deduplicated). Meta.
Retargeting CPL — Cost Per Lead on warm/retargeting campaigns. Usually your cheapest. Meta.
Challenge ROAS — Return On Ad Spend for the paid Imprint Challenge = revenue ÷ spend. Above 1× = profitable before the Day-3 pitch. Meta.
Active ads — ads currently ACTIVE account-wide. Goal 20+ for creative volume. Meta.
Brand & Offer Awareness (AEO) — of 8 AI-discovery queries, how many cite Rich as an AI-for-business authority. 0/8 = invisible to AI.
PR / Earned Media — earned placements per month: podcast guest spots, speaking, bylines, expert quotes, "top expert" lists.
Reach & Impressions — Impressions = total times shown; Reach = unique people who saw them (30d, paid). Organic not yet tracked.
Engagement (organic) — cross-channel shares/comments/likes, prioritized shares > comments > views. Now tracked via GHL (IG+FB real); LinkedIn/YouTube not syncing = a floor.
Time Commitment — avg on-page time + manifesto read-depth: how long people stay and consume.
Grow Retargeting Lists — the pixel/engagement audiences that feed retargeting; CPL + list size.
Stage 2 of 8 · Engage · loading… · Deiss: turn awareness into engagement — "the difference between a glance and a stare"

📖 What these mean

Return-visitor rate — % of sessions from people who've visited before. GA4.
Site engagement rate — % of website sessions (GA4) that were "engaged": lasted >10s, converted, or viewed 2+ pages. On-site behavior — NOT social.
Avg engagement — average session duration (time on site per visit). GA4.
Pages / session — avg pages per visit. Higher = they go deeper (the "stare"). GA4.
Nurture engagement — click-through on the nurture series (GHL workflow emails, ~182K sent/30d, via Mailgun). Opens omitted (MPP-junk). Broadcasts run ~1.2% for contrast. (The raw count of active workflows — 333 — is a sprawl flag on the Nurture-health card, not a KPI.)
Engaged → Front-end ★ — % of engaged people who take the next step toward the paid front-end. Engage's output (not yet instrumented).
ICP (Ideal Client Profile) — how clearly + accurately the target buyer is defined and documented.
Core Messaging — whether the core message + a named "only-through" mechanism are dialed to that buyer.
Consumption depth — how deeply people actually consume: manifesto read-depth, video watch-time, scroll depth.
Email → routed — of email clickers (the engaged), the % routed into an ascension nurture — not just opens/clicks.
Retargeting re-touch — paid reach + frequency against people who already engaged. Meta.
Nurture health — whether the active workflows actually route engaged non-buyers → offer (vs. sprawl).
Community engagement — active participation in the GHL community groups.
Conversation rate — Deiss's "actual conversation": replies, DMs, comments (two-way).
Stage 3 of 8 · Subscribe · loading… · Deiss: get permission to follow up — the lead-capture / opt-in machine · SCOPE: the manifesto is the ONE standing lead magnet today

📖 What these mean

Opt-in conversion ★ — clean manifesto opt-in rate = deduped manifesto opt-ins ÷ manifesto-page sessions (30d). The manifesto is gateless (Rich's red line), which caps it.
Opt-in velocity — deduped manifesto opt-ins in the last 7 days + trend. Event-driven (spikes on challenges, falls off after).
Opt-ins /30d — deduped manifesto gated opt-ins, last 30 days. The clean number — NOT total contacts, NOT GA4 lead-events.
List size — ALL GHL contacts (buyers, unsubscribes, years of history) — a base number, not clean opt-ins.
Mailable list ✓ — the actually-reachable audience: 14,173, VERIFIED from the last full broadcast (Aug 6 Mailgun delivered) = confirms Camilla. Funnel: 40,346 total → ~27,107 deliverable (older) → 14,173 mailable. GHL can't see this (suppressions don't sync).
List growth /mo — net-new contacts as a % of the list, per month.
Deliverability — Mailgun: delivered ÷ (delivered + failed), 30d. Are opt-ins actually reachable?
Lead magnets — count of STANDING (evergreen) lead opt-ins. Today = 1 (the manifesto). Single point of capture = the #1 Subscribe gap.
Opt-in → Front-end — of people who opt in, how many become buyers (real leads or dead?). ≈7.5% adjacent; needs the bridge instrument.
